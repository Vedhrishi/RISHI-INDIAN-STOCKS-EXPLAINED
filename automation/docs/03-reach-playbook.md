# Getting more reach (what actually moves the number)

A Reel stuck around 50 views is rarely a hashtag problem. Treat hashtags as the **last 10%**. Nobody can promise views;
this is a checklist for testing what works for your niche.

## 1. The first 2 seconds decide everything
- Open on the problem, not the logo: a failed screen, a number, a question.
- Burn the hook into the video as big text in the first frame. Most people watch muted.
- Short Reels (15-45 s) that people watch to the end and replay beat long ones.

## 2. The caption's first line is your search keyword
Instagram search reads the caption. Lead with the phrase a real person types ("failed SIP mandate",
"XIRR vs absolute return"), not "Episode 49 Part 2".

## 3. Ask for the actions that count
**Saves, shares/sends and comments** matter more than likes. End every caption with a Save line, a Send-to-someone line and
a one-word Comment prompt (see [caption_template.md](../meta_scheduler/caption_template.md)). Reply to every comment in the first hour.

## 4. Hashtags: niche beats broad
- Mix **2 broad** (`#personalfinanceindia`), **5-6 niche** (`#sipinvestment`, `#zerodhacoin`, `#xirr`) and **1 branded** tag.
- Make them match the Reel. A SIP Reel gets SIP tags, not generic ones.
- Instagram has said hashtags help categorise content rather than boost it, and a handful of relevant tags is enough.
  Test 3-5 against 8-10 for two weeks and keep the winner. Do not paste the same block on everything.

## 5. Consistency and format
- Same slots every day (the scheduler does this for you); keep a recognisable series name.
- Use a clean cover/thumbnail with readable text, and a profile that says in one line who it is for.
- Post the same piece as a YouTube Short and a Facebook Reel; each platform surfaces it to a different audience.

## 6. Measure, then change one thing
Open Insights -> Content, sort by **watch time / retention** and **shares**. Find your top 3 Reels, then copy their hook style.
Change one variable at a time (hook, length, hashtags), give each test 7-10 posts, and keep a note of the result.

## 7. Content rules to stay safe
Education only. Never promise returns or "100% profit". Disclose affiliate links. For finance in India, follow SEBI rules on advice and
influencer disclosure. Labels such as "AI-generated" or "animated" should be used where the platform asks.
