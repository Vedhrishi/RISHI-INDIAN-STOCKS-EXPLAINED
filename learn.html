The kids pack and school licence are made for ages 10 to 16. The full library also suits teens and adults.

No. RISHI is education only. There are no stock tips, no return promises and no paid product placements in the lessons.

English, Hindi and Telugu are ready. Other Indian languages can be made for school and CSR programmes; ask us.

Can we show the videos in assemblies or parent meetings?

Yes. The school licence covers screening anywhere on your campus for one year.
