Questions

Good to know

Can the characters look like our brand?

Yes. Starter and Growth use our characters in your colours. The Signature series includes a custom mascot made only for you.

Who owns the videos?

You can use the delivered videos for your business, on any platform, forever. The Rishi and Priya characters stay ours; custom mascots made for you are yours.

Which languages can you do?

English, Hindi and Telugu as standard. Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali and Gujarati on request.

Do you make finance and investment videos?

Yes, educational ones. We do not make videos with stock tips, return promises or misleading claims. Regulated businesses (for example SEBI, IRDAI or RBI regulated) stay responsible for their own disclosures, and we add any disclaimer you need.

How fast is delivery?

Usually 3 to 5 working days after you approve the script. Growth clients get priority 3-day delivery.

Can I see a preview before paying?

Yes. First-time clients get a free 15-second preview with their logo, and you pay nothing until you approve the script.
